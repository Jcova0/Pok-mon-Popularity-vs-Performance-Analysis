## Purpose

The purpose of this project is to find which Pokémon are loved but weak (buff candidates), strong but overlooked (promotion candidates), or both loved and strong (safe to feature) in order to accurately determine the next balance patch and seasonal promotion.

---

## Goals

This project will run for about six weeks, from November 17 to December 29, 2026. By the end, it will give the design team a data-backed view of which Pokémon to buff and which to feature in the upcoming promotion. The goals are:

1. **Measure the relationship.** By 12/13, define a popularity index and a performance index for Gen 1-9 Pokémon and report how strongly they are correlated.
2. **Explain popularity.** By 12/13, report how much type, generation, and legendary status explain popularity beyond performance.
3. **Identify gap Pokémon.** By 12/14, produce a list of the 15 most overrated and 15 most underrated Pokémon, ranked by the gap between popularity and performance.
4. **Build the dashboard.** By 12/20, publish a dashboard showing popularity and performance for each Pokémon, with filters for type, generation, legendary status, and competitive viability.
5. **Give recommendations.** By 12/27, deliver at least 3 data-driven recommendations on which Pokémon to buff and which to feature, each tied to a specific finding.

## Audience

Kanto Interactive and design team

---

## Story

Currently, the team lacks an evidence-based view of which Pokémon to examine for balance changes or promotional features. This analysis compares fan popularity against competitive performance to sort Pokémon into three groups: loved but weaker Pokémon become candidates for a buff, strong but less popular Pokémon become candidates for the promotion, and Pokémon that are both strong and loved become candidates to return for longtime fans. Featuring a more targeted set of Pokémon could support sales through merchandising and exposure.
